Approaching Support with First-Principles & Root-Cause Logic

"Customer support isn't just about answering tickets—it's about understanding human behavior, identifying systemic friction, and translating customer pain points into actionable system improvements."

1. Root Cause Over Quick Band-Aids

When an issue recurs, resolving the single ticket is only step one. I look for underlying software bugs, UI confusion, or macro gaps that need structural fixes.

2. Empathetic Clarity

High-friction interactions require clarity without jargon. I bridge the gap between technical engineering details and user-friendly, empathetic explanations.

3. Process & Automation Mindset

If a task is performed 50 times a day manually, I evaluate spreadsheet formulas, keyboard shortcuts, or CRM rules to optimize turnaround times.

4. Uncompromising Data Precision

Whether evaluating AI output accuracy or logging Jira tickets, I rely on clear baselines, reproducible evidence, and meticulous attention to details.
