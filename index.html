1. Email Support & Customer Communication

Customer Email

Scenario: Customer charged twice due to a payment gateway timeout during checkout.

Subject: Re: Duplicate Charge Inquiry - Order #84920
To: Sarah Miller <client@example.com>
From: King Philip Mangi - Support Operations
Hi Sarah, Thank you for reaching out, and I sincerely apologize for the confusion caused by the double charge on your recent purchase. I’ve reviewed your transaction history for Order #84920 and confirmed that a temporary processing duplicate occurred due to a brief payment gateway timeout during checkout. I have immediately processed a refund of $49.50 back to your original payment method. Depending on your card issuer, you should see the credit reflected in your account within 3 to 5 business days. Your original order has been fully confirmed and is processing normally. If you have any further questions or if the refund doesn't appear by Friday, please reply directly to this email and I will happily look into it for you. Best regards, King Philip Mangi Support Operations Specialist

2. Engineering Escalation Ticket (Jira Format)

Jira Issue

Scenario: Reporting a repeatable checkout authentication bug affecting Safari users on iOS.

Project: Web App Operations [OPS] Issue Type: Bug Escalation Summary: [iOS Safari] 2FA SMS Verification code field fails to render during checkout Impact: High (Prevents mobile Safari users from completing purchases) Reporter: King Philip Mangi (Support Ops) Description: Multiple customers reporting inability to complete order verification on iOS Safari (v17.4+). The 6-digit OTP modal renders as an empty white box after clicking 'Send Code'. Steps to Reproduce: 1. Add any item to cart and proceed to Checkout. 2. Select Credit Card payment and trigger 2FA SMS verification. 3. Observe modal load on Safari Mobile (iOS 17.4). Expected Result: 6-digit input field appears ready for input. Actual Result: Modal iframe fails to render, throwing JS console error: "Uncaught TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'focus')". Workaround provided to user: Advised switching to Chrome Mobile or desktop browser temporarily. Logs/Payload Attached: console_log_2026_03_12.txt
